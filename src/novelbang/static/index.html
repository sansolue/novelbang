        Where should your novels live?

        Each novel is a folder of plain text files inside this folder. A folder that's backed up or synced (iCloud Drive, Dropbox, a git repository) keeps them safe.

        History

          A version is saved automatically a minute after you stop typing, every few minutes while you write, and before every AI edit or restore. Restoring never loses anything — the current text is saved first.

          Chapter synopses

          The co-writer reads the synopses of earlier chapters to keep the story consistent. Chapters are updated in book order, each one building on the new synopses before it. Nothing is saved until you apply.
